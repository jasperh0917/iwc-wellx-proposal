Section 07 · Journey One — Telemedicine
Journey 01

Talk to a doctor
in five minutes.

The goal

Shift minor consultations from in-person clinic visits to telemedicine — reducing claims cost and improving member convenience.

Expected impact
  • 30–50% shift of minor consults from clinic to telemedicine
  • ₱500–₱1,500 saved per shifted consult (industry benchmark)
  • Higher member satisfaction — 5-min access vs. 1-hour clinic visit
Step 01
Trigger
Member feels unwell. Opens IWC app — home screen shows prominent "Talk to a Doctor" tile. Push notification: "Feeling unwell? Get a doctor in 5 minutes — earn 50 xCoins."
Step 02
Pre-Consult
Member taps tile → quick symptom checker (3–4 questions). App suggests telemedicine for mild cases, in-person for urgent. Member's policy and benefits auto-populate in the background.
Step 03
Consult
Embedded video call with IWC's doctor network. Doctor sees member's policy, prior consults, medications inline. Average call: 8–12 minutes.
Step 04
Reward
Member earns 50 xCoins for completing the consult, plus 25 bonus xCoins for rating it. Digital prescription delivered in-app.

Section 08 · Journey Two — Clinic Visits
Journey 02

Steer the visit
before it happens.

The goal

Steer members toward IWC partner and company-owned clinics — reducing out-of-network costs and increasing volume at preferred facilities.

Expected impact
  • 20–35% increase in visits to company-owned clinics
  • 15–25% shift from out-of-network to partner clinics
  • ₱200–₱800 saved per redirected visit
  • Higher preventive care utilization, reducing long-term claims
Step 01
Discovery
App home screen surfaces nearest IWC clinics with reward amounts displayed. Higher rewards visible for company-owned facilities. Members see options before they need care.
Step 02
Pre-Visit
Member taps clinic → "Check In Here" button generates a one-time QR code. Day-of push reminder: "Don't forget to check in to earn xCoins!"
Step 03
At Clinic
Clinic staff scans QR code at check-in. Member sees instant confirmation. LOA auto-populates — no separate paperwork.
Step 04
Reward
xCoins credited within 24 hours after visit. Notification reinforces the reward. Educational content suggests preventive screenings for double xCoins.

Section 09 · Design Principles
How behavior change happens

Three principles
that do the work.

The platform is designed around behavioral science: reward the right action, make the preferred path the easiest path, and use variable rewards to build lasting habits.
01

Reward the
behavior, not
the outcome.

Members can't always control health outcomes — but they can control behaviors. We reward the right step taken, not the result, so the system is fair and consistently motivating.

02

Make the
preferred path
the easiest path.

The IWC partner clinic is one tap away. Out-of-network requires deliberate effort. When the right choice is also the easiest one, behavior follows architecture — not willpower.

03

Variable rewards
beat fixed
rewards.

Occasional bonus surprises drive far stronger engagement than fixed rates. This is well-established behavioral science, and it's how we sustain interest beyond the early novelty.
